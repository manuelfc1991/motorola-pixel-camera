Capture feedback: now and proposed

Pick a situation. The left phone replays what the app does today; the right phone shows the proposed behaviour. The idea: the shutter button and the gallery thumbnail carry the feedback, so nothing covers the picture and nothing needs reading.

Now

Proposed

Rules behind the proposal

  • No toast for anything that succeeded. The thumbnail changing is the confirmation.
  • No card over the viewfinder. Progress lives on the shutter ring.
  • A setting shows its state as a small chip in the top bar, not a message.
  • Text appears only when you must do something, next to where you do it.

What stays as text

  • Real errors (camera failed to start, recording failed).
  • Guidance during a capture: “Hold steady”, panorama sweep direction.
  • One-line hints that fix a failed attempt, shown for 3 seconds.
