Concentric Dial Concept

Dragging the shutter button leftwards or clicking "Unfurl All Dials" brings up the five nested radial sliders. Each dial operates at its own radius from the pivot point on the right edge.

Live Viewfinder Reaction

Changing ISO and Shutter simulates exposure in real-time on the scene. Changing Focus simulates manual optical blur, and WB warms or cools the scene tone.

Android Architecture Fit

Can be built in our Android app via a custom LumiaRadialDialView.kt utilizing Android Canvas arcs and linked to MediaTek Camera2 manual sensor keys.
